A practical run sheet for two instructors, three laptops, and one bot.

Two 120-minute sessions

Keep every team moving.

Before class

Build with .\gradlew.bat :TeamCode:compileDebugJavaWithJavac. Open the simulator on all three laptops and verify the preconfigured lesson robot configuration. For hardware, load the drive-only profile and test at low power with the bot lifted.

Rotate driver, navigator, and observer/debugger. During each hardware block, one team uses the bot while the other teams run the same lesson OpMode in the simulator. If hardware is unavailable, all teams continue in the simulator. Before each test, have the team predict aloud; after each test, ask what happened and what they would try next.
